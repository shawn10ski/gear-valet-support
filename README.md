## Frequently Asked Questions

### 1. How does AI appraisal and valuation work?
Gear Valet uses vision analysis combined with optical character recognition (OCR) to identify your equipment's make, model, year, and serial numbers. Estimated replacement values are cross-referenced with recent sales comps from major marketplaces like Reverb and eBay Sold listings.

### 2. How do I restore my Pro subscription?
If you reinstalled the app or switched to a new device:
1. Open **Gear Valet**.
2. Tap the **Pro / Upgrade** icon to open the paywall screen.
3. Scroll to the bottom and tap **Restore Purchases**.
4. Sign in with the Apple ID used for the original purchase.

### 3. Where is my equipment data stored?
Your equipment data, photos, and maintenance logs are stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. Your personal data is never sold or shared.

### 4. How do I back up my inventory?
You can create a full JSON snapshot of your collection at any time:
1. Open the dashboard / settings view.
2. Tap **Export Backup Package**.
3. Save the resulting file to your iCloud Drive, Files app, or external storage.
